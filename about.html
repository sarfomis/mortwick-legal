About Mortwick

Mortwick — The Dark Records is an independent history channel. It tells true, documented stories from the past: crimes, disasters, frauds and unsolved deaths, and what happened to the people involved. Every episode starts from historical sources (court records, newspapers of the time, museum archives) and uses public-domain photographs wherever they exist.

How we publish

Full documentaries (8–12 minutes) are published on YouTube, in English with an Italian audio track. Short vertical chapters of the same stories are published on Instagram and TikTok, each pointing to the full episode. Narration voice and some illustrations are AI-generated; this is stated in every video description and post.

Mortwick Publisher (TikTok app)

Mortwick Publisher is a private tool that uploads our own finished short videos to our own TikTok account, @mortwick_thedarkrecords. How it works:

  1. The channel owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and authorizes the app.
  2. The tool takes the next short video from our own library, with its caption and hashtags.
  3. It publishes it to the owner's TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

It does not read, collect or store data about any other TikTok user, and it has no public sign-up. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
